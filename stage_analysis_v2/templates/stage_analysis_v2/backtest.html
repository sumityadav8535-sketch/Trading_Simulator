{# Supertrend rules (shown when ST strategy selected) #}

Supertrend pullback rules

  • Daily Supertrend(14, 3) already bullish
  • First tag only in each bull run (skip repeat wicks)
  • Today’s low tags ST and close stays above it · buy next open
  • Stop = pullback low − 0.25 ATR (not the ST line — avoids noise stop-outs)
  • Trail = Supertrend − 0.5 ATR · exit on ST flip or max hold
  • Skip if stop tighter than 1.8% or wider than 12%
  • Size = risk % of equity, capped at max position %

Dual Supertrend + Minervini (5-year pack)

  • First pullback on ST(14,3) or ST(21,3) · buy next open
  • Minervini template: close > SMA50 > SMA150 > SMA200, 200-SMA rising, ≥25% off 52w low, within 25% of 52w high
  • Only when Nifty is above EMA50
  • Stop = pullback low − 0.25 ATR · skip if stop < 1.8% or > 12%
  • Exit when Supertrend(21, 4) flips bear (or max hold)
  • Risk 2% of equity per trade · max 4 names · 80% cap · no leverage

Cup breakout rules

  • Rounded cup: left-rim high → 12–45% correction → time near the low → recover to ≥90% of the rim
  • Cup lasts 20–180 trading days · V-shaped recoveries are rejected
  • Handle preferred (5–30 days, depth ≤15%) · stop = handle/pullback low − 0.5 ATR
  • Buy when daily close breaks the rim by ≥0.5% on ≥1.1× 20-day volume · fill next open
  • Breakout: close > EMA20, close > SMA50, SMA50 > SMA200, RSI 40–85, gap ≤5%
  • Researched pack: EMA20 trail, 10% risk, 40-day hold · only if Nifty > EMA20 · pause 10 days after 3 losses
